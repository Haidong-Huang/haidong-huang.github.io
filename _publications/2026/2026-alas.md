abstract: >-
  <strong>ALAS</strong> addresses limited environmental transfer and skill reuse in long-horizon human–scene interaction. It disentangles environmental context from self-state dynamics and progressively integrates them through cross-attention, gated fusion, and mixture-of-experts fusion. Evaluations show improved cross-scene transfer and skill composition, faster long-horizon execution, and gains in learning efficiency on HumanoidBench.
