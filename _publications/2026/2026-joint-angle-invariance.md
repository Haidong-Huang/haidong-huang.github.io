abstract: >-
  <strong>Joint-angle invariance (JAI)</strong> reduces the cost of Jacobian-based robot performance evaluation. We identify conditions under which selected manipulability and stiffness indices are independent of the first and last joint angles, enabling reduced-dimensional performance mapping and task-layout optimization. The demonstrated case reduces map-construction time from 163.887 to 1.723 seconds while preserving numerical accuracy.
