abstract: >-
  Our <strong>actuator-constraint-aware teleoperation framework</strong> addresses motion distortion under coupled transmissions, gravity, and motor saturation. It combines coordinated actuator-space projection, gravity compensation, and continuous feasible-reference updates to preserve operator intent and reduce recovery transients. Real-robot experiments show smoother wrist motion, with 73.6% lower average velocity standard deviation than the no-gravity-compensation baseline.
