abstract: >-
  <strong>CoRDE</strong> addresses cross-task interference and unstable expert routing in multi-task manipulation. It combines concept priors with behavioral evidence through confidence-adaptive responsibility inference, while LoRA experts share a frozen diffusion backbone for parameter-efficient specialization. LIBERO and D3IL evaluations show improved task success, more stable expert allocation, and stronger behavioral diversity.
