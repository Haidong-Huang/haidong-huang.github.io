abstract: >-
  We propose <strong>UEPO</strong> to address limited behavioral coverage and distribution shift in offline-to-online reinforcement learning. It combines multi-seed diffusion sampling, dynamic divergence regularization, and trajectory augmentation to improve policy diversity and dynamics-model learning without training multiple policy models. D4RL evaluations report higher aggregate scores than Uni-O4 across locomotion, dexterous manipulation, and kitchen tasks.
