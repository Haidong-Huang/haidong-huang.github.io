abstract: >-
  <strong>MetaWorld-X</strong> separates humanoid skill acquisition from semantic expert orchestration to reduce cross-skill interference. Human-motion-informed experts use imitation-constrained reinforcement learning and training-time latent-model improvement, while a VLM-supervised router refines semantic guidance through action compatibility and task returns. HumanoidBench evaluations show improved task returns and up to 3.8× faster foundational-skill convergence, without online planning at deployment.
