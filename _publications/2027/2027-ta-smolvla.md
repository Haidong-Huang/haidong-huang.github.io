abstract: >-
  <strong>TA-SmolVLA</strong> addresses visually occluded, contact-rich plug insertion by combining bilateral dense force-field tactile tokens with vision, language, and robot state. Its policy-to-control interface converts generated action chunks into filtered, error-limited equilibrium poses for Cartesian impedance control. Real-robot evaluation achieves 80% insertion success (16/20 trials), with dense tactile fields outperforming resultant-wrench inputs in stage-weighted performance across three policy backbones.
