abstract: >-
  <strong>NRBPEM</strong> addresses scale imbalance and sensitivity to extreme values in multi-objective robot trajectory optimization. It combines rank-based normalization from sampled performance distributions with sequential weighted-sum optimization and simplified motion constraints. Simulated polishing and plotting studies show more robust normalization, reduced objective dominance, and faster convergence than min–max normalization while maintaining comparable trajectory performance.
