abstract: >-
  We characterize why passive contact observations can leave post-transition mobility ambiguous and derive identification-query requirements under an ideal projector model. Our <strong>active-confirmation pipeline</strong> combines transition detection, contact-state canonicalization, and targeted motion probes. Controlled MuJoCo noise audits using simulator-ground-truth eligibility report no incorrect geometry decisions, with safe aborts for ineligible cases.
