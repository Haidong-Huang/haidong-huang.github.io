abstract: >-
  <strong>GeoPreserve</strong> addresses the loss of action-relevant geometry in compact 3D diffusion policies. Its <strong>Rewriting–Routing–Recovery</strong> design combines same-source density–depth views, relation-aware conditioning, and multi-scale denoising to preserve geometric distinctions throughout action generation. LIBERO, RoboTwin 2.0, and D3IL evaluations show improved success and behavioral-mode coverage, with only 4.69% more parameters than the Mamba Policy backbone.
