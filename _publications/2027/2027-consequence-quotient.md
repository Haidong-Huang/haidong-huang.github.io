abstract: >-
  <strong>CQ-FM</strong> addresses the mismatch between action-coordinate errors and their task consequences. A learned consequence geometry guides both flow-matching training and safeguarded Gauss–Newton corrections with receding-horizon replanning, without enlarging the VLA backbone. Experiments show higher measured success in simulation and on hardware, including an increase from 69% to 78% across four real-robot tasks.
